## Acrylic Mouse Restraint Tube

**Files:** `AcrylicTube_v2.step`, `AcrylicTube_v2.stl`

**Material:** extruded acrylic, shatter-resistant,lightweight, 1/8 inch wall thickness
**Finish:** clear, glossy both sides
**Dimensions:** 1-1/2 outer diameter, 1-3/8 inner diameter, 3.5 inch length
**Fabrication:** Machined

### Mounting holes
- **Location:** Bottom of the tube; two holes, spaced 1 inch apart
- **Type:** Threaded (tapped) holes, 1/4"-20 UNC
- **Pilot (tap drill) diameter before tapping:** 5.1 mm (No. 7 drill)
- **Fastener:** 1/4"-20 stainless steel socket head cap screw, 5/8 inch length passing through the bottom of the mounting bases
- **Notes:** Hand-tap slowly with cutting lubricant suitable for acrylic, and avoid overtightening to prevent cracking or stripping. Tighten the screws by hand only. Overtightening can crack the acrylic or strip the threads.
